# Complete the editorial and destination review

## Objective

Finish the content refresh with a systematic human review that automated validators cannot replace.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All public copy is reviewed for spelling, grammar, clarity, consistent voice, organization naming, and role terminology.
- [ ] Dates, titles, employment statements, project contribution claims, and image attribution are checked against available evidence and owner knowledge.
- [ ] Internal links resolve in the built output and external links are manually sampled for correct destination and context.
- [ ] HTTP-only links and unavailable destinations receive an intentional update, archival label, replacement, or removal.
- [ ] Owner review is recorded for factual or reputationally meaningful copy.
- [ ] Remaining uncertainties are documented as follow-up items rather than guessed.

## Out of scope

- Guaranteeing that third-party links will never change.
- Rewriting source material hosted by other organizations.
