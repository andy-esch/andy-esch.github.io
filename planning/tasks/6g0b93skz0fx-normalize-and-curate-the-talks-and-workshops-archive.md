# Normalize and curate the talks and workshops archive

## Objective

Preserve the professional speaking record while making it consistent, maintainable, and easier to scan.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Talks and workshops are represented in one authoritative schema with normalized title, venue, location, date, type, description, and optional destination fields.
- [ ] Duplicate, missing, and contradictory entries between existing HTML and data files are reconciled.
- [ ] Typos, malformed markup, and inconsistent date or organization naming are corrected without changing historical meaning.
- [ ] Highlighted appearances are distinguished from the complete chronological archive using the approved content criteria.
- [ ] Unavailable links are replaced, labeled as archival, or removed after review rather than silently left broken.

## Out of scope

- Researching exhaustive speaking history beyond repository evidence without owner input.
- Adding client-side filtering unless archive size and user need justify it.
