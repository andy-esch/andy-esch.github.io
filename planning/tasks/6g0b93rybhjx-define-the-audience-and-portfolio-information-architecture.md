# Define the audience and portfolio information architecture

## Objective

Make the editorial goals explicit before rewriting or reorganizing pages so the site communicates a deliberate professional story.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Primary and secondary visitor groups and the action or understanding desired from each are documented.
- [ ] The intended first-screen message and relative emphasis of current work, selected projects, teaching, and talks are agreed.
- [ ] A small route and page hierarchy identifies homepage sections, detail pages, archives, and navigation labels.
- [ ] Criteria distinguish selected work from comprehensive archival material.
- [ ] Decisions requiring owner preference are reviewed and approved before downstream copy tasks begin.

## Out of scope

- Writing final page copy.
- Implementing templates or CSS.
- Adding a blog or CMS.
