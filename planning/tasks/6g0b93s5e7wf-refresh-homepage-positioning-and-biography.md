# Refresh homepage positioning and biography

## Objective

Update the homepage so a visitor can quickly understand the current professional focus, distinctive experience, and available paths into deeper work.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The role label is consistent across the homepage, shared header, talks, metadata, and other public pages.
- [ ] Introductory copy is concise, current, and written for the agreed primary audience.
- [ ] Career highlights emphasize verifiable responsibility and outcomes rather than a chronological wall of text.
- [ ] Teaching and contact information receive intentional but proportionate placement.
- [ ] All factual claims and subjective positioning receive owner review and approval before publication.

## Out of scope

- Inventing metrics or claims that cannot be supported.
- Creating a resume download without a supplied and approved artifact.
- Changing the visual system.
