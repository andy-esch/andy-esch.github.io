# Responsive and Accessible Experience

**Goal.** Make every public page comfortable and coherent on phones, tablets, desktops, keyboards, and assistive technology while retaining the minimal colored-section aesthetic.

## Why this is its own epic

Responsive behavior and accessibility form a user-facing quality system that spans layout, typography, navigation, embeds, and interaction states. Keeping it separate makes the experience independently testable and prevents architecture or editorial work from obscuring usability outcomes.

## Out of scope

- A new logo or wholesale brand replacement.
- Rewriting the projects and talks archive.
- Decorative animation or JavaScript added without a user need.
