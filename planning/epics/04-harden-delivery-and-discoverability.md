# Harden Delivery and Discoverability

**Goal.** Ensure the published site is reproducible, validated, lightweight, securely delivered, and understandable to browsers, search engines, and link previews.

## Why this is its own epic

Build integrity, supply-chain controls, automated checks, asset performance, metadata, and domain health are cross-cutting operational concerns with their own acceptance evidence and maintenance cadence.

## Out of scope

- Adding analytics or third-party tracking without a separate privacy decision.
- Migrating away from GitHub Pages.
- Server-side controls that GitHub Pages cannot provide.
