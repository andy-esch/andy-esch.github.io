# Refresh Portfolio Content and Navigation

**Goal.** Make the site current, concise, and easy to scan while preserving meaningful depth for visitors who want the complete professional history.

## Why this is its own epic

This work depends on editorial and information-architecture decisions about audience, emphasis, terminology, archive structure, and route names. Those decisions are distinct from implementing a responsive design system or build pipeline.

## Out of scope

- Inventing or overstating professional outcomes.
- Launching a blog, CMS, or ongoing publishing program.
- Implementing an unrelated visual redesign.
