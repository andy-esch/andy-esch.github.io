## Work

Currently building out ML and AI-powered privacy guardrails for data access as a Staff Machine Learning
Engineer at [Ethyca](https://www.ethyca.com/).

Previously, I was a Staff Data Scientist at Premise Data/[Native](https://native.io/), where I designed
and built ML platforms that processed global location data into coherent and actionable points of interest.

Then... at Ipsos' Risk Analytics Division (RAD) where I built data pipelines around spatio-temporal machine
learning models for international development, including rapid response work supporting the World Bank after
the 2020 Beirut explosion.

And before that... I spent five years at CARTO developing spatial data science expertise and created
CARTOframes, a Python package that brought interactive mapping for data science use cases to Jupyter
notebooks.

And before transitioning to data science and software engineering space... I taught high school physics.

## Teaching

**Lecturer at The University of Pennsylvania**

I created and taught [Geospatial Cloud Computing & Visualization](https://github.com/MUSA-509/) at
the [MUSA program](https://www.design.upenn.edu/musa/about) in the Weitzman School of Design,
University of Pennsylvania.

With a strong emphasis on data analysis in Python with SQL and [PostGIS](https://postgis.net/),
students built interactive web applications backed by REST APIs built with
Flask and visualized with Jinja HTML templates.
The student-built web apps told data-driven geospatial stories, often from an urban-planning angle.
Students managed their data in PostgreSQL on [AWS RDS](https://aws.amazon.com/rds/postgresql/) and
[Google BigQuery](https://cloud.google.com/bigquery).

**High school physics teacher**

Two years teaching the wonders of physics as a high school physics teacher at Arlington Public Schools.

## Projects &amp; talks

**desire/lines**

I've been building desire/lines, my personal fitness goals app. It's aspirationally '80s themed with
neon lasers and random '80s easter eggs. Definitely has a map.

It's technically multi-user and I'm considering opening it up to trial users. Written with lots of golang,
typescript/React, protobuf, and terraform. Running on GCP (Cloud Run + PubSub + Firebase + BigQuery)
with a [Neon](https://neon.com/) PostgreSQL database (of course). Fitness activities sync'd with Strava
via webhooks. See rough [architecture diagram](https://github.com/andy-esch/desirelines/blob/main/packages/README.md#data-flow)
for more information. Over-engineered to learn.

Check out the demo site: [desirelines.andyes.ch](https://desirelines.andyes.ch).
